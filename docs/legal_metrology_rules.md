# Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 — Rules Reference

## Core Rules Covered

### 1. Rule 6: Mandatory Declarations

- Every pre-packaged commodity must display mandatory declarations on the principal display panel.
- Declarations must be legible, prominent, and in English or Hindi (Devanagari script).

### 2. Rule 7: Principal Display Area (PDA) & Minimum Font Size

Minimum height of numerals and letters based on Principal Display Area ($A$):

- $A \le 50\text{ cm}^2$: Normal $\ge 1.0\text{ mm}$, Blown/Moulded $\ge 1.5\text{ mm}$
- $50 < A \le 100\text{ cm}^2$: Normal $\ge 1.5\text{ mm}$, Blown/Moulded $\ge 3.0\text{ mm}$
- $100 < A \le 500\text{ cm}^2$: Normal $\ge 2.5\text{ mm}$, Blown/Moulded $\ge 4.0\text{ mm}$
- $500 < A \le 2500\text{ cm}^2$: Normal $\ge 4.0\text{ mm}$, Blown/Moulded $\ge 6.0\text{ mm}$
- $A > 2500\text{ cm}^2$: Normal $\ge 6.0\text{ mm}$, Blown/Moulded $\ge 6.0\text{ mm}$

### 3. Unit Sale Price (USP) Amendment

- For packages $> 1\text{ kg} / 1\text{ L}$, USP in terms of per $\text{kg} / \text{L}$.
- For packages $< 1\text{ kg} / 1\text{ L}$, USP in terms of per $\text{g} / \text{ml}$.
- Must be displayed alongside MRP in equal or prominent size.

### 4. Rule 18: Maximum Retail Price (MRP)

- Price must strictly include phrase `"Inclusive of all taxes"`.
- Overcharging or dual MRP violation tracking.
